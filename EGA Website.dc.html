About EGA

What we do, every semester.

EGA is one of the University of Delaware's registered Graduate Student Organizations. Our job is to make graduate school in CEHD less isolating and more navigable, and to make sure the college hears from students before decisions get made.

Newsletter

Published across the fall and spring, covering events, announcements, and other important information for CEHD graduate students.

Social events

Low-pressure ways to meet people outside your cohort, from coffee-and-donut mornings to grad student socials with visiting speakers.

Education workshops

Sessions chosen from what students actually ask for, on job markets, methods, and life after the degree.

Student voice

We collect feedback through needs assessments and priorities surveys, then advocate for it with college leadership.

Finding somewhere to live

Newark has on-campus graduate options, a UD-only roommate group, and a wide off-campus market. Each one has its own catch, so read all three before you sign anything.

There are more on-campus housing options for graduate students this year. Before you commit, read through the new graduate housing contract.

Off campus

Plenty of our graduate students rent nearby, and you can scout most of those buildings on Google Maps. Some people live in Elkton, MD, which is a short drive to Willard Hall and often cheaper.

Many students and professors also commute in from other parts of Delaware, and even from Philadelphia, since the Newark train station makes the trip workable without living in Newark.

Before you sign, ask:

  • Utilities. What is included, and what lands on you?
  • Deposit. How much, and when is it due?
  • Guarantor. Do they require one, and will they accept an assistantship letter instead?
  • The lease itself. Read the whole rental agreement, including the renewal and early-termination clauses.

Watch out for rental scams. Third-party groups are not screened by the university. When you are looking for a roommate or a sublet, deal only with people who have a @udel.edu address, and never send a deposit before you have seen the unit and read the lease.

Open position

Be the first-year representative.

First-year students see the gaps most clearly, and the board needs someone who is living them right now. The first-year representative brings the concerns of the incoming cohort to EGA, helps shape the workshops and socials we plan, and gets a fast introduction to how CEHD actually works. No prior experience with the organization is expected.

Fill in the interest form → Ask a question first

What the role involves

  • The representative serves a 1.5-year term, beginning in the fall of their first year and ending the following fall. They act as a liaison for first-year students, addressing concerns, promoting events, sharing resources, planning activities, and later mentoring the incoming representative through guidance, collaboration, and support.

Eligibility

  • A member of the first-year cohort
  • A full-time doctoral student in SOE or HDFS
